Questions before you order

Is the 10% off on every direct order?

Yes, on every WhatsApp or phone order, for delivery or pickup. No coupon code and no minimum.

How do I pay?

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) or cash when the food arrives. For office orders above ₹3,000 we can share a GST invoice.

Do you deliver to my area?

We deliver within about 4 km: Sadashiv, Narayan, Shaniwar and Kasba Peth, Deccan, Erandwane and parts of Kothrud. Share your location on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Send a photo on WhatsApp within an hour. We'll replace the dish or refund it by UPI the same day. No forms, no chatbot.

Can you handle a big office or party order?

Yes, up to 60 thalis with a day's notice. Message us the count and time and we'll send a quote.

Will you spam me on WhatsApp?

No. We only message about your order, plus the Sunday special if you ask for it.
